# Stage 2, Parts 3–5 — Public Stats, Builder, Auto-Build (compact plan)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Finish stage 2 on top of parts 1–2: public server-rendered stats pages, the manual squad builder with FPL rules, auto-build and AI squad review for credits.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-stage-2-epl-analytics-design.md`. **Part 2 outputs used here:** tables `predictions`, `model_evaluations`, `fpl_player_status`, `fpl_game_settings`, `player_season_prices` (`price_source = 'FPL'`), `ForecastService.getStatus()` («enabled» = bar passed).

## Global Constraints

- Public pages (`/players`, `/players/[id]`, `/fixtures`, `/how-we-calculate`) need no login, read **our database only** (never API-Football per visit), cache for 1 hour (`unstable_cache`, revalidate 3600).
- Forecast numbers are shown **only when `ForecastService.getStatus().enabled`**; otherwise show form and stats without a forecast and say why.
- Prices: only `priceSource = 'FPL'` rows with `updatedAt` newer than 3 days count; otherwise «no price» and the budget check is skipped.
- Squad rules come from `fpl_game_settings` (squad 15, starting 11, budget 1000 tenths, ≤ 3 per club, positions 2/5/5/3 with min/max play 1/1, 3/5, 2/5, 1/3), never hard-coded.
- Builder and AI/auto-build require login; paid features charge via `IWalletService.spendOnFeature` after generation, cached per (user, kind, key, gameweek) in `AiReport` (docs/server-architecture.md «Paid AI features»). Prices: squad review 30, auto-build 300 (`src/entities/ai/model/ai-pricing.ts`). A cached result is free within the gameweek; when the forecast changed since, show «forecast updated» and a paid recompute.
- Project rules: Controller → Service → Repository, DI in `src/shared/lib/container.ts`, no `any`, `import type`, no `console.log`, lower-case commit subjects.

## Review Focus

1. Squad validation accepts exactly the FPL rules: size, positions, club limit, budget, formation limits, captain ≠ vice, both in the starting XI. (Task 4 tests)
2. Auto-build never returns a squad that breaks a rule, honours locked and excluded players, and fails without charging when infeasible. (Task 6 tests)
3. Auto-build/squad review charge only after success, once per key; no price data → auto-build refuses with a clear reason. (Task 7 tests)
4. Public pages with missing data (no price, no forecast, unknown player id) render without errors. (Task 2/3 tests)
